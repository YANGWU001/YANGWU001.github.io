I am an AI Research Scientist at Axon in Boston. I work on trustworthy natural language processing and large language models, combining data-efficient training, human-centered feedback, and domain-adaptive reasoning. The applications I care about are legal reasoning, biomedical discovery, personalized education, and public safety.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Worcester Polytechnic Institute in May 2026, advised by Xiaozhong Liu. My dissertation is *Advancing the Proactive Capacity of LLMs in Cost-Sensitive Domains*. Before that, I earned an M.P.S. in Analytics from Northeastern University and a B.S. in Material Engineering from Wuhan University.

Research
======
I lead work on data-efficient LLM training, proactive assistants, multimodal systems, and generative AI. Recent papers appear at ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, AAAI, WWW, and CIKM. A full list, including manuscripts under review, is on the [publications](/publications/) page.

Selected directions:

* **Proactive collaboration.** Assistants that decide when to speak, rehearse reasoning ahead of time, and stay useful under a tight latency budget.
* **Cost-sensitive learning.** Reward-oriented data selection and expert-in-the-loop acquisition when labels and domain experts are expensive.
* **Domain reasoning.** Legal question answering, biomedical discovery, personalized tutoring, and multimodal video understanding.
